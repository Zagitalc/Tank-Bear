# Tank Bear 0.2.4-beta

Android friend-testing prerelease — build 6.

## Download and update

Download **Tank-Bear-0.2.4-beta.apk** from Assets. Requires Android 8 or newer.
Open it on your phone and install as an update to Tank Bear Beta to keep your
existing app data. Updates are manual. Location permission remains optional;
you can choose locations on the map. No account or app key is needed.

## Fixes since 0.2.3-beta

- Start and destination search fields stay above the keyboard. The keyboard's
  Search action submits the lookup and dismisses the keyboard.
- Postcodes are consistently spaced/capitalised, with autocorrection disabled
  for place entry. Place results and the first fuel result are brought into view.
- A fixed View results control returns to the current fuel-search outcome.
- Best total, Pump price and Least driving select the leading eligible station,
  update its highlighted map route and scroll to its card. Re-tapping the active
  sort works too. Open now filtering is respected.
- Litres and MPG scroll above the keyboard, including near the page bottom.
  Done closes editing while keeping the entered values.

## Verification and limits

30 unit tests, focused emulator checks, debug/beta builds and beta lint passed
during development. The owner confirmed the postcode, sort and Car keyboard
fixes on a Samsung phone. Full TalkBack and broader device review remain open.

Prices, opening hours and savings are estimates. Nearby distances are
straight-line distances and nearby fill estimates exclude driving costs.
Journey comparisons use routed driving costs. Northern Ireland routing is not
covered. Report problems with the app version and reproduction steps; avoid
private start/destination coordinates in public reports.

Fuel price data: Fuel Finder/ICS. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors,
displayed through MapLibre using OpenFreeMap tiles. Place search uses Geoapify.
